Website-backed business directories for JobRadar

Source: Overture Maps Foundation, Overture Places.
https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/places/
https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/#places

JobRadar selects business names, categories, public websites, addresses,
and rounded coordinates. The Germany index selects records with a German
address and excludes closed places and social profile links. The earlier
Leipzig and Bielefeld extracts cover creative businesses with websites.
